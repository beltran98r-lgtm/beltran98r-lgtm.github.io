How it works

1. Employees scan the QR and submit weekly availability.
2. The schedule engine compares coverage to minimum staffing.
3. Callouts trigger a backup candidate list instead of a staffing scramble.
4. Employees can offer and accept compatible shift swaps instantly.

Smart callout protection

Rather than labeling individual employees as “likely to call out,” StaffSync adds a staffing buffer to historically fragile shifts and keeps a voluntary backup pool.

Demo rule: Friday evening and weekend morning shifts target +1 reserve employee when availability allows.

Manager rules

• Never schedule outside submitted availability.
• Respect full-time/part-time preferred hours.
• Prioritize voluntary backup-pool employees for callout coverage.
• Require manager approval for swaps that create overtime or skill-mix issues.
